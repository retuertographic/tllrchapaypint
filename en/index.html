How it works

Six stages. One single file.

The vehicle is checked in once and never leaves the system until the customer signs for it.

  1. 1

    Check-in

    Register the vehicle by plate, along with customer and insurance details. The customer signs the check-in form digitally.

    Digital signature
  2. 2

    Damage assessment

    Damage is marked on the vehicle diagram and documented with photos and video of its actual condition on arrival.

    Visual diagram
  3. 3

    Courtesy vehicle

    Where applicable, a replacement vehicle is assigned from the fleet, with handover, mileage and return date on record.

    Fleet under control
  4. 4

    Repair

    Work moves through stages. Every status change and every new photo updates the file instantly.

    Traceability
  5. 5

    The customer checks it themselves

    The portal updates live: current stage, progress photos and estimated handover date. Fewer calls.

    Live
  6. 6

    Handover

    Final checklist, signed customer acceptance and the file closed with its full documentary history.

    File closed
